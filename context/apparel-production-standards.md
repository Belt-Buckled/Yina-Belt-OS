# Days Like These — Apparel Production Standards

**Status:** Active release 1.0  
**Approved:** 2026-08-22  
**Logo authority:** [approved Option 4 Drive master](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HMddC1vxwXjN7-n5RbrGiwARCZFDFWri/view?usp=drivesdk)

## Purpose

This standard governs logo placement, scale, color and production method across T-shirts, sweatshirts,
hoodies, pajamas, caps, totes, inside-neck labels and woven patches.

It is a placement and production standard, not a garment pattern, full technical pack, digitizing file or
screen-print separation.

## No-drift rule

The approved Drive master is the sole authority for logo geometry. Use only the exact-master assets in the
active apparel package. Preserve letterforms, internal spacing, stacking and proportions.

Do not retype, redraw, trace, thicken, compress, stretch or rearrange the wordmark. If embroidery or another
process cannot reproduce the approved silhouette, increase the physical size or switch to a woven patch,
screen print or heat transfer. Never modify the logo to suit a machine.

The embedded SVG and PNG assets contain exact raster extractions. They are not designer-original vectors.
Replace them only when the original vector artwork is supplied and visually verified against the approved
Drive reference.

## Placement hierarchy

- **Primary:** center-front and full-back applications use the exact primary stacked mark.
- **Secondary:** left chest, sleeve and small leg placements use the exact small-scale stacked mark.
- **Utility:** inside-neck and narrow woven labels use the exact horizontal mark.
- **Accessory:** caps, patches and totes use the appropriate exact stacked configuration and require a
  physical proof.
- Do not combine two logo placements on the same visible face without collection-specific approval.

## Adult target widths

| Placement | S–M | L–XL | 2XL+ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Center front | 9.5 in | 10.5 in | 11.5 in |
| Full back | 11.5 in | 12.5 in | 13.5 in |
| Left chest | 3.25 in | 3.25 in | 3.50 in |
| Outer sleeve | 2.25 in | 2.25 in | 2.50 in |

Center-front art begins 2.5–3.0 inches below the collar seam. Full-back art begins 3.5 inches below the back
collar seam. Width is the controlling dimension; height follows from the locked aspect ratio.

## Youth and pajama target widths

| Application | Youth XS–S | Youth M–L | Youth XL / Adult |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Center front | 7.0 in | 8.0 in | 9.0 in |
| Full back | 8.0 in | 9.5 in | 10.5 in |
| Left chest | 2.5 in | 2.5 in | 2.75 in |
| Pajama outer leg | 4.0 in | 4.5 in | 6.0 in |

Coordinated family sets preserve the same optical placement rather than the same absolute size. Approve one
adult and one youth strike-off before a full run.

## Production methods

### Screen print

Preferred for hero placements. Use the stacked mark at 3.25 inches wide or larger. Use an underbase on dark
garments and verify that it does not expand the silhouette. Match ink from a physical drawdown.

### Heat transfer

Preferred for short runs. Use transparent, high-resolution exact-master art and a matte finish unless a
collection specifies otherwise. Confirm fabric temperature tolerance.

### Embroidery

A sew-out on the actual fabric and machine is mandatory. The initial target minimum is 2.75 inches wide,
with an approximate finished minimum stroke of 1.0 mm and open counter of 1.5 mm. These are starting
constraints, not permission to alter the mark. If the approved shape does not hold, increase size or use a
woven patch.

### Woven patch or label

Use when fine logo details exceed embroidery limits. Preserve the complete approved silhouette and reject
stitch-driven redrawing.

## Approved garment and logo colors

- Warm Ivory garment: Rich Black, Oxblood or Cobalt mark.
- Rich Black garment: White or Warm Ivory mark.
- Oxblood garment: Warm Ivory or White mark.
- Cobalt garment: Warm Ivory or White mark.
- Muted Blush garment: Rich Black mark.
- Marigold garment: Rich Black mark.

Tonal embroidery, metallic thread, puff ink, foil and distress treatments require separate physical approval.

## Required release checks

Before production, confirm the exact asset filename, locked aspect ratio, seam-based placement, adult and
youth scale, underbase behavior, embroidery counters, physical ink/thread swatches and wash-test results.
Archive a photo of the approved sample with the vendor, blank, size and run date. Written approval is
required before production.
