# About the Business

**Brand name:** Not chosen yet. Naming it is a 90-day priority.

## The offer

Custom design application on physical products. I design the artwork, then apply it to the item.
This is not reselling. Reselling is a different business and it is explicitly out of scope.

Today the product is apparel: DTF transfers pressed onto blanks such as Gildan 5000s. Secondary product
under consideration is the digital file itself, selling the PNG or the transfer rather than the finished
garment.

## Who it is for

Stated target: moms of young kids, buying party favors and party decor for kids' parties.

Actual demand so far has come from somewhere else. The one finished project was a custom concert shirt
for adults, and the only unsolicited inbound this business has ever received came from posting that reel.
Paid customers to date are family.

**Open tension, deliberately unresolved:** the stated ICP and the proven demand are not the same market.
Both are live. The 90-day sales number will settle which one is real. Do not quietly collapse one into
the other.

## Channels and licensing posture

| Channel | Status | What sells there |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Marketplace | In use | Finished shirts, including concert and fan-art designs. Local pickup to date, open to shipping. |
| Etsy | Possible, not started | Digital PNG and transfer files only. Not finished fan-art shirts. |
| eBay | Undecided, leaning no | Nothing |

The split is intentional. Artist-referencing designs stay on Marketplace to keep exposure contained.

**Recorded caveat:** this lowers visibility, not liability. Etsy runs automated takedown enforcement that
Marketplace does not, which is a difference in how fast a listing gets pulled, not in whether the use is
licensed. Selling digital files of artist-referencing artwork is the higher-exposure move, not the safer
one. The decision stands, made with open eyes.

## Money

Revenue is not tracked anywhere. It is a receipts pile. The payment rail for local pickup was never
named and is still an open item.

Known unit economics from the one completed project: two-pack of Gildan black blanks at $9.91,
transfers at $32.63, one outsourced 5X print. Net result was a $100 loss, before counting time and labor.
