# Days Like These — Wordmark Context

**Status:** Approved visual identity direction  
**Approved:** 2026-08-22  
**Selected concept:** Option 4 — Two Eras

## Decision

Option 4 is the approved wordmark for **Days Like These** and is now the standard for future visual identity work.

The lockup pairs a condensed sans-serif treatment for **DAYS LIKE** with a high-contrast editorial serif treatment for **THESE**. This intentional two-style construction expresses the brand's “two eras” idea: the millennial mom's memories and the moments she is creating for her family now.

## Approved visual behavior

- Primary stacked lockup: `DAYS LIKE` above `THESE`.
- Horizontal lockup may be used where a wide format is required.
- Approved reference colorways include oxblood on warm ivory, cobalt on warm ivory, and white on rich black.
- The mark may be adapted for woven labels, embroidery, screen printing, foil, stamps, packaging, profile avatars, video covers, and watermarks.
- Small-format use must be tested for legibility; use a simplified, production-ready variant when the full lockup becomes too fine.
- Applications should stay aligned with the approved **Ticket & Thread** visual baseline.

## Guardrails

The original comparison boards establish the approved concept and application direction. The responsive production package now supplies outlined SVG masters, transparent PNG exports, print-ready PDFs, exact color tokens, clear-space rules and minimum-size guidance.

Before manufacturing, require a vendor proof at the intended physical size. Embroidery must be sampled on the actual fabric and machine because thread weight, fill and open counters vary by production method.

Do not substitute Option 3 — The Maker Label. Do not stretch, skew, rotate or rearrange the approved configurations.

### Production palette

- Warm Ivory: `#F5EFE4`
- Rich Black: `#111111`
- Oxblood: `#7A1E22`
- Cobalt: `#163F8C`
- Marigold accent: `#D89A2B`
- Muted Blush accent: `#E6B3B0`

### Minimum sizes

- Horizontal: 32 mm print / 160 px digital
- Stacked: 24 mm print / 120 px digital
- Compact circle: 48 px digital
- Small-scale / micro: 18 mm print / 96 px digital; embroidery proof required

Clear space is 1X on all sides, where X equals the cap height of “DAYS LIKE.”
