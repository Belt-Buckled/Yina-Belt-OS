## Read this first

This strategy describes a **personality-led lifestyle account**, not the apparel shop. It carries the
same name as the product brand and it is not the same thing. See "Unresolved" at the bottom before you
use anything here on customer-facing work.

## Purpose

Build an account that becomes an opportunity engine. Brand deals, partnerships, platform revenue,
affiliate, YouTube, future products or services. Follower count is a milestone, not the scorecard.

**Stated financial goal:** contribute toward **$15,000/month in additional household income.** This is a
household number over an open horizon, not the $1,000 quarter goal.

## What the account is

A woman with ideas, taste, attention to detail and a tendency to do the most, turning ideas into real
experiences while figuring out the next chapter of her life and income.

**Explicitly not:** a mom account, a DIY account, a hosting account, a sobriety account, or a
make-money-online account. Those topics can appear. None of them owns the brand.

## Brand feel

Warm. Observational. Intentional. A little polished, not sterile. Funny when it naturally is. Honest
without oversharing. A little "of course she did all that." A little "I would absolutely do that too."
Occasionally deeper than people expect.

## The four content lanes

| Lane | What it holds | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Projects and ideas | House projects, things being made, Halloween costumes, party prep, custom items, decorating, testing an income idea | Action, progress, visual payoff |
| 2. Hosting and experience-making | Christmas party, birthdays, Thanksgiving, Fourth of July, decor, food setup, guest comfort, what worked and what changes next time | Care about how people feel in the space |
| 3. Life in progress | Trying to create income, testing ideas, projects that stall, ideas that do not work, goals and transitions | Relatability without becoming a hustle account |
| 4. Personal perspective and story | Reflective thoughts, the 2:00 replay, reinvention, underdog themes, past stories, sobriety if and when she frames it | Makes it a person, not a project board |

## On-brand test

A post is on-brand if it does at least one of these:
- Shows a project, idea or process moving toward an outcome.
- Shows the extra attention to detail that is naturally hers.
- Tells a story with an emotional, visual or practical payoff.
- Shows an effort to make life better, prettier, easier, more meaningful, more efficient or more profitable.
- Gives viewers access to her thought process.
- Creates the feeling "I want to see what she does next."

Off-brand: random footage with no point of view, story, useful context or connection to the larger brand.

## Series to test

- **I Decided To...** Flexible format. Anything that starts with a decision and moves to an outcome.
- **Christmas Build.** Seasonal tentpole. The 2026 concept is a nostalgic 90s Christmas. Audience follows
  the build, not just the reveal. The party stays a real family event first and content second.
- **The 2:00 Thoughts.** Personality series based on the moment external noise quiets and the internal
  replay starts.
- **Worth the Extra?** Tests whether a detail, purchase, process or extra effort was actually worth it.

## Cadence and workload

- **Four to six meaningful posts per week** during the first 30 days. Explicitly do not jump to three
  posts per day.
- Each week ideally mixes one project post, one personality post, one hosting or detail post, one
  life-in-progress post.
- Normal posts should land at **20 to 60 minutes** of production once the workflow improves. Four hours
  per post is not a sustainable normal. Occasional heavier edits are fine when the content earns it.

## Content functions to track

Reach posts bring new people in. Connection posts make people feel they know her. Series posts make
people come back. Proof-of-personality posts keep it from feeling generic.

Track the function, not just the count.

## Privacy and boundaries

- Children are not the center. They may appear without faces when appropriate.
- Marriage appears lightly. The relationship is not a recurring content engine.
- AJ's background may be referenced selectively as part of an underdog or life-story theme. His story is
  not the core brand.
- Her own face can be explored gradually, not forced.
- Privacy protections get stricter as the account grows.
- Sobriety is not a content pillar until she decides how to frame it.

## Production comfort boundary

Close-up hand footage is not a default when nails are not done. Press-ons are acceptable. Otherwise use
wider framing, alternate angles, object-focused shots, sleeves, tools or tripod shots.

This is a hard constraint on shot lists, not a preference.

## First four priority posts

1. **The 2:00 Replay.** Connection and personality. B-roll plus voiceover or text. Face not required.
   Hook: "Every day around 2:00, the external noise quiets... and then my brain hits replay."
2. **Apparently I Do Too Much.** Proof of personality. Concrete behavior, not explanation.
   Hook: "Apparently I do too much. I just don't want to hand you a bag of chips at my house."
3. **Christmas Story Begins.** Series launch. Establishes the party as a real tradition with history.
4. **Current Project With a Deadline.** Project and reach. Must be a genuinely active project.
   Hook: "This has been on my list way too long, and Christmas just gave it a deadline."

## What not to do

Do not center the children. Do not build around the marriage. Do not force sobriety into a pillar early.
Do not turn every income idea into a public income challenge. Do not chase a narrow niche too early.
Do not spend four hours editing every post. Do not make follower count the only scorecard. Do not let
production turn real family events into production sets.

## Resolved 2026-08-29 — this strategy governs the brand

The conflict this file originally carried is closed. **Days Like These is the umbrella lifestyle brand
and this document governs it.** The shop lives inside the brand as one of the opportunities it
showcases.

That means this file is no longer quarantined. Its lanes, series, cadence, privacy rules and production
boundaries are live for content work.

**What it does not govern:** the shop's own plan. The $1,000 gross by 2026-11-20, the pricing, the
listings and the customer messages still run off `context/about-business.md` and
`context/priorities.md`. Those are unchanged by this decision.

**Cadence, in practice.** This doc says 4 to 6 meaningful posts per week for the brand. `priorities.md`
commits to 3 posts per week for the shop. They are not the same count of the same thing, and a single
asset can serve both. Do not merge the numbers. Track shop posts against priority 3 and brand posts
against the function mix above.

**Still open, do not invent answers:**

1. **Where the product enters the content.** Undecided. Which posts carry a CTA, how often, and what a
   shop post looks like inside a lifestyle feed. Raise it before the first batch goes out.
2. **What comes off the plate.** Two plans is two plans.
3. **The handle.** If Days Like These is the umbrella, the main handle probably should not contain
   "shop". See `decisions/log.md`, 2026-08-29.
