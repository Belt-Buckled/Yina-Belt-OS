# About Me

**Name:** Yina Belt.

I design custom artwork and apply it to physical products. Right now that means apparel, printed with
DTF transfers on blanks, pressed by hand. I have run one full project end to end: a custom concert shirt,
front and back, in 3X and 5X, with one piece outsourced to a print vendor and the rest pressed myself.

I am a solo operator. No team. The business does not have a name yet.

## How I work

I think out loud and I work the problem in the open. I ask a lot of short, specific questions until the
thing is right, and I check my own assumptions as I go. I would rather ask whether a print is too small
five times than press it wrong once. I say what I actually think, including when I do not want to do
something.

## What eats my week

Sourcing. Comparing transfers and blanks across vendors, weighing unit cost against shipping to find the
real landed price. I have no preferred vendor yet, so I re-run that comparison from scratch on every
project. Initial design is the second time sink.

## The pattern I need broken

On my first real project I netted a hundred dollar loss, because cost was never settled before the work
was quoted. Then I had to figure out how to politely go back and ask for more money. Sourcing and pricing
are the same wound. Fix the landed cost number and the pricing problem mostly goes away.
