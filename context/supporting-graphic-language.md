# Days Like These — Ticket & Thread Graphic Language

## Components

1. **Ticket frames** — offers, edition releases and event information.
2. **Stitched borders and dividers** — tactile structure and separation.
3. **Edition markers** — real numbered runs, collections and documented occasions.
4. **Date stamps** — meaningful real dates; never fabricate archival history.
5. **Label structures** — product, care, package and operational information.
6. **Ribbon and tape shapes** — one emphasis point per composition.
7. **Controlled speckle texture** — 3–8% opacity; never obscure type or product detail.

## System rules

- Use no more than two supporting elements in one composition.
- Hierarchy: logo → message → information → graphic detail.
- Supporting elements may use Rich Black, Oxblood or Cobalt. Marigold and Muted Blush are accent fills only.
- Keep the logo clear-space zone free of borders, stitches, stamps and texture.
- Edition numbers and dates must represent something real.
- Do not combine ticket notches, stitched borders, ribbon, stamps and texture all at once.
- Avoid fake distressing, sepia effects, scrapbook clutter and literal sewing illustrations.

## Editable assets

Source SVGs retain editable text and the CSS variables --dlt-ink and --dlt-paper. Outlined SVGs are vendor-safe. PNG previews are for placement and review.
