# Days Like These — Production Color System

## Hierarchy

- **Warm Ivory** is the default surface and negative-space color.
- **Rich Black** is the default ink, long-copy and high-contrast color.
- **Oxblood** is the warm emotional brand color for keepsake, family and tactile applications.
- **Cobalt** is the energetic brand color for digital emphasis, apparel and contemporary contrast.
- **Marigold** and **Muted Blush** are accent colors only; they should not dominate a composition.

Recommended overall ratio: Ivory 45%, Black 25%, Oxblood 12%, Cobalt 12%, Marigold 3%, Blush 3%.

## Accessibility

Use only the approved pairings in the production board and token file for normal-size digital text. Never place Oxblood on Rich Black, Cobalt on Rich Black, Marigold on Warm Ivory, or Muted Blush on Warm Ivory for essential text.

## Print production

The supplied CMYK values are starting conversions, not press guarantees. Paper stock, ink system, coating and printer profiles change the result. Require a contract proof for packaging and a strike-off for fabric.

Do not assign Pantone numbers from a screen conversion. Match against a current physical Pantone book with the selected vendor, then record coated, uncoated and textile references separately.

## Physical applications

- Natural cotton, kraft and uncoated ivory stock may serve as the Warm Ivory surface after proofing.
- For embroidery, use thread-book matches and stitch samples; do not specify thread from HEX alone.
- For foil, treat Oxblood, Cobalt and Marigold as color intentions and approve the actual foil swatch.
- Rich Black may require a vendor-specific rich-black build for large fields; small type should use 100K-only unless the printer advises otherwise.
