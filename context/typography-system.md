# Days Like These — Typography System

**Status:** Approved  
**Approved:** 2026-08-22  
**Direction:** Option 1 — Editorial Ticket

## Font family

### League Gothic — display and utility

Use for campaign headlines, social titles, ticket numbers, edition labels, packaging section names and short calls to action.

- Prefer uppercase and Regular weight.
- Track between 0 and +20 depending on size.
- Keep headlines short; never use it for paragraphs.
- Recommended line height: 0.88–0.98.
- [League Gothic on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/League+Gothic)

### Bodoni Moda — editorial and keepsake

Use for emotional headlines, pull quotes, occasion names, product names and large price statements.

- Use Regular or Medium for large display text.
- Use Bold only when physical production needs more weight.
- Avoid small sizes and long paragraphs because its fine strokes lose clarity.
- Recommended line height: 0.92–1.10.
- [Bodoni Moda on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bodoni+Moda)

### Instrument Sans — body, interface and information

Use for body copy, descriptions, captions, navigation, buttons, pricing details, care instructions and operational information.

- Regular 400 for body copy; Medium 500 for labels and navigation; Semibold 600 for buttons.
- Recommended body line height: 1.40–1.60.
- Use sentence case for prose and uppercase only for short utility labels.
- [Instrument Sans on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)

## Hierarchy

| Level | Font | Weight | Case | Typical size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero / campaign | League Gothic | Regular | Uppercase | 56–120 px |
| Editorial headline | Bodoni Moda | Regular–Medium | Title or sentence case | 40–80 px |
| Section heading | League Gothic | Regular | Uppercase | 28–48 px |
| Product name | Bodoni Moda | Medium | Title case | 24–40 px |
| Utility label | League Gothic or Instrument Sans | Regular / Medium | Uppercase | 11–16 px |
| Body copy | Instrument Sans | Regular | Sentence case | 16–20 px |
| Small print | Instrument Sans | Regular | Sentence case | 12–14 px |
| Button / CTA | Instrument Sans | Semibold | Sentence or uppercase | 14–16 px |
| Price | Bodoni Moda or League Gothic | Medium / Regular | As written | 24–56 px |

## Signature pairing

The standard treatment uses League Gothic for the setup or utility phrase, Bodoni Moda for the emotional or occasion-led phrase, and Instrument Sans for supporting information. Do not give all three families equal visual weight: one leads, one supports and Instrument Sans carries the information.

## Production guidance

- The approved logo remains outlined artwork. Do not rebuild the wordmark by typing these fonts.
- Install or load the font families separately for brand communications and templates.
- Use the Google Fonts releases unless a future licensed desktop package is explicitly approved.
- For embroidery, foil, stamps and small tags, proof Bodoni Moda at intended size; use League Gothic or Instrument Sans when fine serif strokes cannot reproduce reliably.
- Fallback stacks: `Arial Narrow, Arial, sans-serif`; `Didot, Bodoni MT, Times New Roman, serif`; and `Inter, Arial, sans-serif`.
