# Days Like These · Birthdays — Aiden’s Fifth

**Campaign label:** Edition 001 · Level Five  
**Status:** Preproduction; event date not yet recorded  
**Role:** First documented Days Like These event and first complete favors-and-decor proof set  
**Campaign owner:** Yina Belt

## Decision

Aiden’s Fifth is the first complete application of the Days Like These brand system. It documents one real
family birthday build across custom apparel, handmade favors, decor, garage transformation and game-zone
details.

This is brand proof, not a new service category. Days Like These is not offering party planning. The
customer-facing offer remains **handmade favors, decor and custom apparel**.

- Hero fifth-birthday shirt and coordinating family apparel.

- Inquiry-ready product and listing copy.

## Content sequence

The rollout stays event-relative until the birthday date is confirmed:

1. **T–4 · Signal:** introduce Edition 001 without revealing the full build.
2. **T–3 · Build:** show apparel, favors, stitch/press work and the maker process.
3. **T–2 · Transform:** reveal black drapes, garage zones and game details.
4. **T–1 / event · Reveal:** publish the full collection, event energy and Aiden’s reaction.
5. **T+1 · Proof:** recap the day, publish product details and invite inquiries.

## Capture and privacy

Required capture includes garage before/after, black-drape installation, worktable process, apparel, favors,
the complete collection, game-truck context, skee-ball/giant-game activity, Aiden’s first reaction, approved
family interaction, artifact details and founder reflection.

Do not expose the home address, street number, school information or unapproved guest children. Capture
children from behind or crop them when permission is absent. Preserve natural skin tone and honest family
energy.

## Brand authority

The approved Option 4 Drive master remains the sole authority for logo geometry. Campaign templates use the
exact extracted silhouette. Do not retype, redraw, trace, thicken, compress, stretch, restack or substitute
fonts.
