# Days Like These — Social Identity Kit

## Included templates

- Two 1080 × 1080 profile avatars.
- Square edition announcement and memory/quote posts.
- 1080 × 1350 product feature and event recap posts.
- 1080 × 1920 announcement, behind-the-scenes and product Stories.
- 1080 × 1920 product and event Reel/TikTok covers.
- White, black and oxblood transparent watermark masters.

## Platform rules

- Keep essential Story/Reel information inside the marked central safe zone.
- Use the compact avatar only for profile images; never force the full horizontal logo into a circle.
- Limit each design to two supporting graphic elements.
- Cover headlines should remain under seven words whenever possible.
- Put the logo at the end of the hierarchy, not above the content message.
- Watermarks should appear at 55–80% opacity over imagery and must maintain contrast.
- Use real edition numbers and dates only.

## Editing

Source SVG files retain editable text. Install League Gothic, Bodoni Moda and Instrument Sans before editing. Outlined SVG files are vendor-safe but their template copy is no longer editable as text.
