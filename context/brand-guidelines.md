# Days Like These — Official Brand Guidelines

**Status:** Active release 1.0  
**Compiled:** August 2026  
**Positioning:** Contemporary nostalgia  
**Visual baseline:** Ticket & Thread  
**Approved wordmark:** Option 4 — Two Eras

## Decision

The official brand guide consolidates the approved verbal identity, exact logo authority, typography, color,
supporting graphic language, photography, layout, social, packaging, apparel and production governance into
one operating reference.

Use the guide for direction and cross-system rules. Use the latest active application package for editable
templates and production files.

- **Descriptor:** Handmade favors, decor and custom apparel.
- **Primary line:** Made for days like these.
- **Hang-tag line:** Made by hand for days like these.
- **Primary buyer:** one millennial-mom buyer across her own and her children’s occasions.
- **Collections:** Birthdays, Concerts, Christmas and Everyday.
- **Handle:** `@daysliketheseshop`
- **Primary hashtag:** `#dayslikethese`

## No-drift authority

The approved Drive file `days-like-these-option-4-wordmark-system.png` is the sole authority for logo geometry.
Do not retype, redraw, trace, thicken, compress, stretch, restack or substitute fonts. If production cannot
reproduce the mark, change the size, placement or production method.

The active SVG releases embed exact raster extractions and are not designer-original vectors. Replace them
only after an original vector is supplied and visually verified against the approved reference.
