# Before You Meet Yina

A character note provided during intake, written by someone who knows her. Kept because it is the most
accurate description of how she actually operates, and because the operating rules in
`context/about-me.md` and `CLAUDE.md` are derived from it directly.

Personal material. If Yina wants it out of the repo, delete this file — the derived rules stand on their own.

Before You Meet Yina
 A warning label, respectfully.
She notices everything. Tone changes. Weird energy. The thing you said three conversations ago that
doesn't quite match what you're saying today. Don't worry - she may not mention it immediately. She's still
processing.
She is a planner. Not in a cute little 'I have a calendar' way. I mean there may be a master plan, a backup
plan, a running note, a checklist, and a completely separate system for maintaining the system. If you
casually suggest hosting 40 people, understand that somewhere in her brain a spreadsheet has already
opened.
She loves hard, shows up hard, and puts a ridiculous amount of thought into making things special. Which
means she also notices when that level of care isn't reciprocated. You cannot repeatedly give her crumbs
and expect her not to eventually conduct a full internal audit.
Also, do not confuse capable with unlimited capacity. She can manage the kids, the house, meals,
projects, holidays, relationships, and seventeen other invisible things - which unfortunately sometimes
convinces everyone, including her, that she should be able to manage an eighteenth.
She can also be extremely hard on herself. Give her one exhausted, impatient day and she might briefly
launch an investigation into whether she has ever been a good mother in her entire life. Evidence to the
contrary will be extensive. The investigation will proceed anyway.
She likes details. If she asks what happened, 'basically...' is probably not going to cut it. And if she gave
you the details already, for the love of God, do not make her explain them again.
She's sentimental underneath all that competence, too. Things mean something to her. Traditions mean
something. Milestones mean something. The feeling of an experience matters just as much as how it looks.
And finally: she is very much a work in progress, but she is the kind who actually does the work. She
reflects, adjusts, rebuilds systems, tries again, and occasionally turns a minor inconvenience into a full
operational improvement initiative.
 So no, you don't need to be afraid of her.
 Just come correct. And maybe bring a notebook.

## What this means for how the AIOS works

Derived rules, now enforced in `CLAUDE.md`:

1. **"Basically" is not an answer.** She asked for detail because she wants detail. Summarizing a thing
   she asked to understand is a failure, not a kindness.
2. **Never make her repeat herself.** If she has already given the details, they go in the files and get
   read from the files. Re-asking is the single most annoying thing this system could do.
3. **Surface contradictions rather than smoothing them.** She notices inconsistency and may not say so
   immediately. She is still processing. Papering over a conflict does not hide it, it just delays it and
   costs trust.
4. **Capable is not unlimited.** She can carry seventeen things, which convinces everyone including her
   that she can carry an eighteenth. Before adding to her plate, say what comes off it.
5. **When something fails, keep it a systems failure.** The $100 loss on the concert shirt was a pricing
   process that did not exist, not a character flaw. She will supply the self-criticism without any help.
   Do not add to it.
6. **The feeling of a thing matters as much as how it looks.** Traditions, milestones, details. This is
   why contemporary nostalgia is not a marketing abstraction for this business. It is her actual value
   system pointed at a customer who shares it.
