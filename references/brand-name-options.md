# Brand Name Options, Round 1

Drafted 2026-08-22. Decision due **Friday 2026-08-28** (Week 1, Priority 1).

## The constraints the name has to survive

1. **It cannot mean "shirts."** Party favors, decor and digital transfer files are all in scope. A name
   built on apparel boxes out two thirds of the business.
2. **It cannot mean "kids."** The proven buyer bought a kids' birthday shirt *and* an adult concert shirt.
   She buys for her child and for herself. A kids-only name cuts the adult half of the same customer.
3. **It cannot mean "Yina personally"** unless that is a deliberate choice. A personal name is honest and
   works fine, but it makes the business harder to hand off or sell later.
4. **It has to be sayable at a birthday party.** Word of mouth inside a mom circle is the current
   distribution channel. If it cannot be repeated out loud once and remembered, it fails.

## Direction A — The maker is the brand

Personal, warm, credible. Easiest to build trust with, hardest to ever separate from.

| Name | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Made by Yina** | Plain, honest, covers every product line. Says a person made this, which is the actual differentiator against print-on-demand. | Generic construction. Hard to own in search. |
| **Yina & Co.** | Lets the business outgrow one pair of hands later. | "& Co." is crowded. |
| **The Belt Press** | Real craft word (heat press), real surname, works for apparel and favors both. | Reads slightly masculine, slightly industrial. |

## Direction B — The craft is the brand

Grounded in the actual process. Distinctive, but needs explaining once.

| Name | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm Press** | The literal mechanism, and it sounds like a feeling. Covers apparel and anything else pressed. | Could read as a bakery or a publisher. |
| **Press & Party** | Says both halves of the business in three syllables. Apparel and favors, one name. | Locks in "party" harder than she may want. |
| **Second Coat** | What a maker does when the first pass is not good enough. Says care. | Reads as painting, not fabric. |

## Direction C — The occasion is the brand

Widest coverage. Favors, decor, shirts, digital files all fit under an occasion.

| Name | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Party of Ours** | Warm, a little witty, covers favors, decor and apparel without naming a product. Sounds like something a mom says. | Restaurant association. |
| **The Favor Table** | Concrete image every mom of young kids knows. Instantly explains the favors line. | Narrows toward favors, away from apparel. |
| **Little Occasions** | Covers birthdays, holidays, concerts, everyday. Product-agnostic. | "Little" pulls toward kids only. |

## Direction D — The buyer insight is the brand

Built on the actual discovery: one buyer, two occasions, buying for her kid and for herself.

| Name | Why it works | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Ours to Wear** | Names the kid-and-me duality without saying "mommy and me." Apparel-leaning but not exclusive. | Leans apparel, weaker for favors. |
| **Mine & Theirs** | The exact purchase pattern, stated. Memorable, slightly unusual. | Needs a beat to understand. |
| **Both of Us** | Simplest expression of the same idea. Very sayable. | Very common phrase, hard to search. |

## Recommended three to take to availability check

1. **Party of Ours** — widest product coverage, warmest, most repeatable out loud.
2. **Warm Press** — most distinctive, most ownable, says craft without saying shirts.
3. **Made by Yina** — safest, fastest, zero explanation needed. The fallback that always works.

## Before committing, check all three

- Instagram handle, Facebook page name, Etsy shop name
- Domain, .com if possible
- USPTO TESS search for a live mark in apparel and party goods classes
- Plain Google search for a local business already using it

**None of these have been checked for availability.** They are creative options only. Availability and
trademark screening is the next step, and it is the step that kills most first-choice names.
