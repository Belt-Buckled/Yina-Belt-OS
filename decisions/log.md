# Decisions Log

Append-only record of meaningful decisions and why they were made. `/level-up` Phase 2 (Method interview) writes scoped automation specs here. You can also append manually whenever you decide something worth remembering.

**Format per entry:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Decision:** what was decided.

**Why:** the reasoning, constraints, and what would change your mind.

**Alternatives considered:** what else was on the table.

**Owner:** who's accountable.
```

Keep it terse. Future-you will thank present-you for capturing the *why*, not just the *what*.

## 2026-08-22 — Fan-art shirts stay on Facebook Marketplace, digital files may go to Etsy

**Decision:** Concert and artist-referencing designs are sold only on Facebook Marketplace. Etsy, if used,
carries digital PNG and transfer files rather than finished fan-art shirts. eBay is undecided, leaning no.

**Why:** To contain licensing exposure by limiting where artist-referencing work is listed.

**Recorded caveat:** this lowers visibility, not liability. Etsy runs automated takedown enforcement that
Marketplace does not, which changes how fast a listing gets pulled, not whether the use is licensed.
Selling digital files of artist-referencing artwork is the higher-exposure move, not the safer one.
Decision stands, made with open eyes. What would change it: a cease and desist, a platform strike, or a
shift toward original designs that carry no licensing question at all.

**Alternatives considered:** listing everywhere, or avoiding artist-referencing designs entirely.

**Owner:** Yina Belt.

## 2026-08-22 — Brand naming sequenced ahead of sales and posting

**Decision:** Picking the brand name is priority 1 of the 90-day window, ahead of the $1,000 sales target
and the 3x per week posting cadence.

**Why:** Both other priorities are partially blocked without a name. 39 posts cannot be posted "about the
brand" when the brand is unnamed, and listings have no consistent identity to sell under.

**Alternatives considered:** selling first under a personal name and naming the brand later, once the
market picks between the kids' party favor ICP and the adult concert apparel demand.

**Owner:** Yina Belt.
