The honest read

01 is the most beautiful and the most expensive. Woven labels, printed ribbon, deckle stock and embossing all carry minimum orders and lead times. That is money and waiting spent before the first sale, in a quarter whose entire goal is the first $1,000. It also photographs beautifully and scrolls badly, and your channel is a phone screen.

03 is cheap in materials and expensive in skill. With no graphic device to hide behind, every photo has to be good. That is a new craft to learn on top of making, selling, posting and planning two events.

02 is the one I would pick, and I did not expect to. It is the only direction you can execute tonight, in Canva, with a local print shop and no minimum order. Big flat type is the only thing on this page that survives a thumbnail. And it is the only one where the brand gets louder as the party gets louder, instead of asking the party to be quieter.

The hybrid worth considering. Take 02's type and boldness as the everyday system, and borrow 01's ticket and stamp as the two physical objects. You would get something that scrolls hard and still feels kept in the hand. That is not a compromise, it is picking the loud thing for the screen and the warm thing for the object.

Reminder: typography here is a stand-in. Bodoni Moda, Archivo and Cormorant Garamond are showing direction, not chosen faces. Nothing is committed until you say so.
