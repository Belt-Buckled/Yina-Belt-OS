Every tile here is rendered in code, not photographed. The two dashed tiles are shot briefs rather than fake images, because a placeholder photo would only teach you to like a picture you cannot reproduce. Those two are the shots to actually take at Aiden's party.

This palette is wider than the five values proposed earlier. Six, not five, and it adds marigold and a dusty slate blue. Brick sits almost exactly where faded cherry did, so the accent instinct holds. Marigold and slate need a job or they will drift: marigold for embroidery, foil and small warm marks, slate for denim and cool product surfaces only. Neither one gets to be a background.

The ticket is the strongest idea on this board. Date, occasion, made for, by hand, with a serial number. It carries the date device, it works for a concert and a sixth birthday equally, and it references the *form* of a ticket without reproducing any artist's or venue's marks. That keeps it clear of the licensing line.

Nothing here is committed. Typography is a stand-in: Bodoni Moda for the display and Jost for the letterspaced marks.
